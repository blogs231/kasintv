How it works

  1. Choose conversion mode: Text to Morse or Morse to Text.
  2. Type or paste your input. For Morse use dot (.), dash (-), space between letters and slash (/) between words.
  3. Click Convert — results appear instantly. Copy or download as needed.

Common Use Cases

Learning Morse for ham radio or education
Generating puzzles or codes for games
Decoding simple messages from text dumps
Teaching telecommunication history and encoding

Pro Tips

  • Use uppercase for text-to-Morse for consistency.
  • Separate words in Morse with '/' when decoding.
  • Test on short samples before converting long texts.

FAQ

Which characters are supported?

Letters A-Z, digits 0-9, common punctuation (.,?,!, ,) and space/word separator '/'.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — all conversion happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

How do I indicate a word gap in Morse?

Use a slash '/' between words (e.g., '... --- ... / .-.. --- ... -').

Can I download the result?

Yes — copy the result or use your browser's save/copy features. This tool is client-side and supports download via the page UI.
