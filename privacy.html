1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the KaSin TV website at kasin-tv.com, including its calculators, converters, generators and informational pages. It does not control the privacy practices of websites or services operated by other organizations.

2. Tool inputs and browser processing

Many KaSin TV tools perform their main calculation or transformation in your web browser. For those tools, the text or numbers you enter are not intentionally transmitted to KaSin TV for processing unless a page clearly states otherwise.

Some tools may store preferences or working content in your browser using local storage. That data remains under the control of your browser and can generally be removed by clearing site data.

Please avoid sensitive information: Do not enter passwords, banking credentials, private identification numbers, confidential records or other highly sensitive data into any online utility unless you fully understand how it works.

3. Information that may be collected

Technical and usage data

Our hosting, security, analytics or advertising providers may automatically receive limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested URL, approximate location derived from IP, timestamps and diagnostic data.

Information you send

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content and attachments.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies, local storage, tags or similar technologies may be used for site operation, preferences, measurement, security and advertising.

4. How information may be used

  • Operate, maintain and secure the website.
  • Diagnose errors and improve tools, performance and usability.
  • Respond to support messages, corrections and privacy requests.
  • Understand general traffic and usage patterns.
  • Display, measure and limit advertising where applicable.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect users, the website and others from misuse.

5. Cookies, Google advertising and third parties

KaSin TV uses Google advertising services. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize and measure ads, subject to your location, consent choices and Google settings.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites policy. Advertising preferences may be managed through Google My Ad Center.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser. Some features or preferences may not work as expected after cookies or local site data are disabled.

6. Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, security, analytics, advertising or communication. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect rights and safety, or address fraud and abuse.

We do not claim to sell contact messages or tool inputs. Service providers may process technical or advertising data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, legal requirements, security and dispute resolution. Actual periods can vary by data type and provider.

7. Your privacy choices

  • Use browser controls to block or remove cookies and local site data.
  • Adjust advertising personalization through provider settings.
  • Use private browsing or privacy-focused browser features where appropriate.
  • Contact us to ask about information you previously sent directly to KaSin TV.

Depending on where you live, local law may provide additional rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. These rights can be subject to exceptions and verification requirements.

8. Security and children's privacy

We use reasonable measures intended to protect the website and information under our control. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

KaSin TV is a general-audience utility website and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information may contact us to request review or deletion.

9. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website, providers or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

For privacy questions or requests, email kasintv94@gmail.com with the subject Privacy Request.
