Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between SVG and PNG downloads?

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) is a vector format that can be scaled to any size without quality loss, making it ideal for printing. PNG is a raster format that's perfect for web use and digital documents but has fixed dimensions.

Which barcode format should I use for my product?

For retail products in North America, use UPC-A. For international retail, use EAN-13. For shipping, logistics, or internal use, Code-128 is recommended. For industrial applications, Code-39 is a good choice.

Can I use custom colors for my barcodes?

Yes, you can customize both the bar color and background color. However, for optimal scanning, ensure there's sufficient contrast between the bars and background. Black bars on white background is the most reliable combination.

What are the optimal dimensions for printing barcodes?

For most applications, a height of 1-1.5 inches works well. Bar width should be appropriate for the scanning distance - thinner bars for close scanning, thicker bars for distance scanning. Our tool provides preset options for common use cases.

Can I add text below the barcode?

Yes, you can add an optional caption that will appear below the barcode. This is useful for displaying the encoded data in human-readable form or adding additional information.

Are the barcodes I generate scannable?

Yes, all barcodes generated with our tool follow industry standards and include proper start/stop characters and checksums where required. We recommend testing with multiple scanners to ensure compatibility.

Is there a limit to how many barcodes I can generate?

No, there are no limits. You can generate as many barcodes as you need, completely free. All processing happens in your browser, ensuring your data remains private and secure.
