Image Upload

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software to use this QR code scanner?

No, our QR code scanner works entirely in your web browser. There's no need to download or install any additional software. Just allow camera access when prompted for real-time scanning.

What types of QR codes can this scanner read?

Our scanner can read all standard QR code formats including URLs, plain text, contact information (vCard), email addresses, phone numbers, SMS messages, Wi-Fi credentials, and geographic coordinates.

Why can't my camera access the QR code?

Common issues include: insufficient lighting, camera too far from QR code, QR code damage, or browser permission issues. Ensure good lighting, hold steady 6-12 inches away, and check that you've allowed camera access.

Is my data secure when using this QR code scanner?

Yes, all scanning and processing happens locally in your browser. We don't store your images, camera feed, or decoded content on our servers. Your data remains completely private and secure.

What image formats are supported for upload?

We support all common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF. For best results, use high-quality images with clear, undistorted QR codes.

Can I scan QR codes from screenshots or printed materials?

Yes, you can scan QR codes from both digital screenshots and printed materials. For screenshots, use the upload feature. For printed materials, use the camera scanning feature.

Does this work on mobile devices?

Yes, our QR code scanner is fully optimized for mobile devices. It works on smartphones and tablets across all major browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.
