Online Case Converter

Fix capitalization in one click with uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case conversions.

Choose the right capitalization style

Uppercase is useful for short labels, lowercase normalizes inconsistent text, title case capitalizes each word and sentence case capitalizes the beginning of sentences. Always review proper names and style-specific small words after automatic conversion.

Can I convert long text?

Yes. The conversion runs locally, so articles, notes and lists can be processed without uploading them.

Will formatting be retained?

Line breaks and punctuation remain in place while letter capitalization changes.
