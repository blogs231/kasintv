Accurate character and word counting

This tool reports the total string length, a count excluding whitespace, words separated by whitespace and the number of text lines. It is useful when writing search titles, meta descriptions, advertisements, application forms and platform posts with length limits.

Does punctuation count?

Punctuation and symbols count as characters. The “no spaces” metric removes all whitespace but retains punctuation.

Does the counter save my text?

No. Counting is performed locally in the current browser tab.
