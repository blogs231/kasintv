Salary Calculation in Nepal - Important Information

Tax-Free Threshold

In Nepal, the first NPR 400,000 of annual income is tax-free for individuals. The calculator applies this exemption when calculating your tax liability.

Provident Fund

Most organizations in Nepal deduct 10% of basic salary for provident fund. Both employee and employer typically contribute 10% each.

Tax Slabs in Nepal

Nepal uses progressive tax rates:

  • First NPR 400,000: 1%
  • Next NPR 100,000: 10%
  • Next NPR 200,000: 20%
  • Above NPR 700,000: 30%

Social Security Tax

Nepal has a social security scheme where employees contribute 11% of their basic salary, though implementation varies by organization.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the tax-free income limit in Nepal? +

In Nepal, the first NPR 400,000 of annual income is tax-free for individuals. This means you don't pay any income tax on the first NPR 400,000 you earn in a year.

How is provident fund calculated in Nepal? +

Provident fund is typically calculated as 10% of your basic salary. Both employee and employer contribute 10% each, making the total contribution 20% of basic salary. However, only the employee's contribution is deducted from your salary.

What are the current income tax slabs in Nepal? +

As of current Nepal tax laws:

  • First NPR 400,000: 1%
  • Next NPR 100,000 (400,001-500,000): 10%
  • Next NPR 200,000 (500,001-700,000): 20%
  • Above NPR 700,000: 30%
These rates apply to your taxable income after deductions.

Are there any additional deductions from salary in Nepal? +

Yes, besides income tax and provident fund, there may be other deductions including:

  • Citizen Investment Trust (CIT) deductions
  • Social Security Fund contributions
  • Insurance premiums
  • Loan repayments
  • Other voluntary deductions

How does marital status affect tax calculation? +

In Nepal, married individuals may be eligible for certain tax benefits, particularly if they have children. The tax calculation may consider family status when determining applicable deductions and exemptions.

When is the best time to use a salary calculator? +

A salary calculator is particularly useful when:

  • Evaluating a new job offer
  • Planning your monthly budget
  • Negotiating a salary increase
  • Understanding your compensation package
  • Planning for tax filing

Is the calculator accurate for all types of employment? +

While our calculator provides accurate estimates for standard employment in Nepal, certain employment types may have different tax treatments:

  • Government employees: May have different allowance structures
  • Contract workers: May be subject to different withholding rates
  • Commission-based employees: Tax calculation may differ
Always consult with a tax professional for specific advice.
