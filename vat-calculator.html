How VAT Calculator Works

Our VAT calculator is a simple yet powerful tool that helps you quickly determine Value Added Tax amounts in Nepali Rupees. Here's how it works:

Adding VAT to Net Price

When you want to calculate the total price including VAT from a net price:

  1. Enter the net price (price without VAT)
  2. Select the appropriate VAT rate
  3. Choose "Add VAT to Net Price"
  4. Click Calculate to see the VAT amount and gross price

Removing VAT from Gross Price

When you have a gross price and want to find out the VAT amount and net price:

  1. Enter the gross price (price including VAT)
  2. Select the appropriate VAT rate
  3. Choose "Remove VAT from Gross Price"
  4. Click Calculate to see the VAT amount and net price

The calculator uses the formula: VAT Amount = Price × (VAT Rate / 100) and automatically handles both calculation directions based on your selection.

VAT in Nepal - Important Information

Standard VAT Rate

The standard VAT rate in Nepal is 13% as per the current tax laws. This applies to most goods and services unless specifically exempted or subject to a different rate.

VAT Registration

Businesses with annual turnover exceeding NPR 20 lakh (2 million) must register for VAT in Nepal. Registration can be done through the Inland Revenue Department.

VAT Exemptions

Some goods and services are exempt from VAT in Nepal, including basic food items, educational services, healthcare services, and agricultural products.

Filing VAT Returns

Registered businesses must file VAT returns on a monthly basis by the 25th of the following month. Late filing may result in penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VAT in Nepal? +

VAT (Value Added Tax) in Nepal is a consumption tax levied on the supply of goods and services at each stage of production and distribution. The current standard VAT rate in Nepal is 13%.

Who needs to register for VAT in Nepal? +

Businesses with an annual turnover exceeding NPR 20 lakh (2 million) are required to register for VAT in Nepal. Businesses below this threshold can voluntarily register.

What is the difference between net price and gross price? +

Net price is the price before VAT is added. Gross price is the final price that includes VAT. For example, if an item costs NPR 1000 net and VAT is 13%, the gross price would be NPR 1130.

How often do I need to file VAT returns in Nepal? +

VAT-registered businesses in Nepal must file returns on a monthly basis, by the 25th day of the following month. For example, the VAT return for January must be filed by February 25th.

Are there penalties for late VAT filing in Nepal? +

Yes, there are penalties for late filing of VAT returns in Nepal. The penalty is typically a fixed amount plus interest on any outstanding VAT payments. It's important to file returns on time to avoid these penalties.

Can I claim VAT refunds in Nepal? +

Yes, if the VAT you have paid on business purchases (input tax) exceeds the VAT you have collected on sales (output tax) in a given period, you can claim a refund from the Inland Revenue Department.

What items are exempt from VAT in Nepal? +

Some items exempt from VAT in Nepal include: basic food grains, educational services, healthcare services, agricultural products (unprocessed), books and newspapers, and public transportation services.
