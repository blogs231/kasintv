Understand repetition in your writing

Word frequency shows how often each normalized word appears. Density is the word count divided by the total number of words. The results can reveal accidental repetition, dominant topics and vocabulary variety.

Is keyword density a ranking target?

No fixed density guarantees search performance. Write naturally for readers and use this table as an editing signal, not as a target to repeat phrases.

How are words counted?

The analyzer converts text to lowercase and recognizes letters, numbers, apostrophes and hyphens. It lists the 50 most frequent terms.
