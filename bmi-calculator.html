Adult BMI ranges

Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5-24.9 is typical healthy range, 25-29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obesity range.

Important note

BMI can be less accurate for athletes, children, pregnant people and older adults. Use it as a simple reference.
