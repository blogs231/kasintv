How to report a problem

For a useful bug report, please include:

  1. The full URL of the affected page.
  2. What you entered and what result you expected.
  3. What actually happened, including any error message.
  4. Your device and browser name, if the problem appears technical.
Safety reminder: Do not email passwords, banking details, government identification numbers, private documents or other sensitive personal information.

Privacy requests

Use the subject line Privacy Request and describe what you need. We may ask for enough information to understand or verify the request, but you should not send unnecessary sensitive data.

Please read our Privacy Policy for information about website data, advertising technologies and your choices.

Before sending a message

  • We do not offer account recovery because KaSin TV tools do not require a user account.
  • For urgent professional matters, contact the relevant qualified professional or authority.
