Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software to use this barcode scanner?

No, our barcode scanner works entirely in your web browser. There's no need to download or install any additional software. Just allow camera access when prompted for real-time scanning.

What types of barcodes can this scanner read?

Our scanner can read most common linear barcode formats including Code-128, UPC-A, UPC-E, EAN-8, EAN-13, and Code-39. We're continuously working to add support for more barcode types.

Why can't my camera read the barcode?

Common issues include: insufficient lighting, camera too far from barcode, barcode damage, poor image quality, or browser permission issues. Ensure good lighting, hold steady 6-12 inches away, and check that you've allowed camera access.

Is my data secure when using this barcode scanner?

Yes, all scanning and processing happens locally in your browser. We don't store your images, camera feed, or decoded content on our servers. Your data remains completely private and secure.

What image formats are supported for upload?

We support all common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF. For best results, use high-quality images with clear, undistorted barcodes.

Can I scan barcodes from screenshots or printed materials?

Yes, you can scan barcodes from both digital screenshots and printed materials. For screenshots, use the upload feature. For printed materials, use the camera scanning feature.

Does this work on mobile devices?

Yes, our barcode scanner is fully optimized for mobile devices. It works on smartphones and tablets across all major browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.
